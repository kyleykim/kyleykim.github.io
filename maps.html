05 · Personal study · Remote sensing

Mapping the Hurricane Maria blackout in San Juan

A self-directed exercise to build my remote sensing skills: mapping the loss of nighttime light around San Juan, Puerto Rico, between August 2017 and Oct. 4, 2017, after Hurricane Maria. Rather than placing before and after images side by side, I tried to show the difference itself, using an upsampled NDUI approach instead of the native VIIRS Day/Night Band radiance alone.

Type
Personal study, not published
Data
  • VIIRS Day/Night Band nighttime radiance
  • August 2017 and Oct. 4, 2017
Method
Upsampled NDUI, used to show change directly
